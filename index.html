Good questions

Frequently asked

Is an online e-stamp legally valid?+
Yes. E-stamps are issued through the official government e-stamping system and carry a unique ID and QR code. Anyone — a court, bank, landlord or authority — can verify them online instantly. They are the modern, tamper-proof replacement for traditional stamp papers.
What do I need to provide?+
For individuals: your CNIC number and phone number, plus the purpose and value of the stamp. For companies: your NTN and order details. Send it all on WhatsApp — we do the rest.
What exactly does the PKR 2,000 cover?+
Our complete service: generating your official e-stamp on the government portal, drafting your document (rent agreement, affidavit, etc.), formatting it, and delivering a print-ready PDF on WhatsApp. The government stamp duty itself — the face value of the stamp — is separate and charged at official rates with no markup.
How do I pay?+
Right in the WhatsApp chat — we'll share our bank / mobile wallet details (JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank transfer). You pay after your order is confirmed, and receive your e-stamp as soon as payment clears.
How fast will I get my e-stamp?+
Most orders are completed within 30 minutes of confirming your details and payment — often faster. Since we're online 24/7/365, that holds at midnight and on holidays too.
Do you only serve Islamabad and Rawalpindi?+
The twin cities are our focus right now, and where we can serve you best. If you're elsewhere, message us anyway — depending on your document, we may still be able to help.
I'm an overseas Pakistani — can you help?+
Absolutely. Everything happens on WhatsApp, so it works from anywhere in the world. Power of attorney documents for overseas Pakistanis are one of our most common orders.
